Privacy Policy for WuZhu

Effective date: August 18, 2026

WuZhu ("the App") is developed by an individual developer, Hanyu Liu ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information on Android devices.

Summary: WuZhu does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no internet permission and never communicates with any server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

WuZhu does not collect any personally identifiable information. It does not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, does not display advertising, and does not require or create user accounts.

2. Accessibility Service Usage

WuZhu uses Android's Accessibility Service API to read the on-screen accessibility tree of the foreground app, solely to detect when a short-form video feed (e.g. YouTube Shorts, Instagram/Facebook Reels, TikTok) is on screen. This detection happens entirely on your device, in memory, in real time. WuZhu does not record, log, transmit, or persist any content read through the Accessibility Service — it is discarded immediately after each check.

3. Local Data Storage

WuZhu keeps a local, on-device count of how many short videos were covered each day, stored using Android's standard SharedPreferences mechanism. This count never leaves your device and is never shared with us or any third party.

4. Permissions

WuZhu requests only the BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE permission, which Android requires to operate an accessibility service. WuZhu does not request internet, storage, location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions.

5. Third Parties

WuZhu does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or trackers. No data is shared with any third party, because no data ever leaves your device.

6. Children's Privacy

WuZhu does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect any information from anyone.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
